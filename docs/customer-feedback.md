## Demo script (20 min)

### Intro (2 min)
"I built a system that ingests your security policies and auto-fills questionnaires. More importantly, it learns from your approved answers and reuses them across future questionnaires — while flagging answers that become stale when policies change."

### Demo (5 min)
1. Knowledge base: upload policies → show chunks parsed
2. Questionnaire: show questions parsed from XLSX
3. Generate: click Generate Answers → show AI-filled answers with citations and confidence
4. Review: approve one answer → explain it goes into the answer library
5. Export: download filled XLSX

### Discovery questions (13 min)

**Volume & pain:**
- "How many security questionnaires does your company receive per year?"
- "How much time does your team spend on them?"
- "What's the biggest pain: speed, consistency, compliance, or knowledge loss?"

**Organizational memory signals:**
- "How often do you answer the same question for different customers?"
- "When a policy changes, do you review your previous questionnaire answers?"
- "Do you ever answer the same question differently in different questionnaires? Why?"
- "If you could build a library of approved answers and reuse them, would that help?"
- "What happens if someone on your team leaves — do you lose security knowledge?"

**Pricing & adoption:**
- "Would you use this across multiple questionnaires per quarter?"
- "What would make this worth paying for: speed, consistency, or knowledge preservation?"
- "Would you pay $300–500/month? $1,000/month? $2,000/month?"
- "Who else needs to approve answers? Is a multi-user approval workflow important?"

**Competitive:**
- "Do you use Vanta, Drata, or another GRC tool?"
- "What's missing from those tools?"
- "Would an answer library + stale-answer detection be a reason to switch?"
